# Changes summary — 24 November 2026

1. **Bank Transfer — Amend** — Recent bank transfers can be amended (banks, amount, date, reference, remark). Choose a required amendment reason. Bank balances update when amount or banks change. Each change is stored in amendment activity (who / when / reason / old→new) and audit logs. Open a row for activity details, or use **Amend** on the row.
2. **Withdrawal Banker — Upload UTRs review** — After validating a UTR upload file, the review step shows separate **Validated** and **Not Validated** tables with Player ID and Amount (Withdrawal ID is no longer shown in these tables).
